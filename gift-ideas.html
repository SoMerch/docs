Gift Ideas

Gift Ideas is a curated content module that provides themed corporate gift inspiration for every occasion - from onboarding packs and employee gifts to partner campaigns and event giveaways.

What are Gift Ideas?

Gift Ideas is a collection of curated articles that suggest specific product combinations, themes, and approaches for corporate gifting scenarios. Each gift idea is designed around a real use case, with practical product recommendations, decoration options, and budget considerations.

How it works

Gift Ideas are organized by tag and category. Teams can browse by theme (e.g., "sustainable gifts", "welcome kits", "conference swag"), budget range, or recipient type. Each article includes:

  • Curated product selections for the specific scenario
  • Decoration and branding suggestions
  • Packaging and presentation ideas
  • Budget-friendly alternatives and premium options

Available languages

Gift Ideas content is available in English, German, and French.

Use cases

  • Employee welcome and onboarding packs
  • Holiday and seasonal corporate gifts
  • Conference and event giveaways
  • Partner appreciation and client gifts
  • Internal brand campaign merchandise
