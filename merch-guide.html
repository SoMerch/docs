Merch Field Guide

The Merch Field Guide is a categorized collection of in-depth guides that cover corporate merch topics - from product selection and decoration methods to fulfillment logistics and program management.

What is the Merch Field Guide?

The Merch Field Guide is a structured content module organized by category and subcategory. Each guide provides practical, actionable information for teams managing corporate merch programs. Topics range from product-specific guidance (e.g., choosing the right hoodie fabric) to operational topics (e.g., managing multi-country fulfillment).

How it works

The Field Guide is organized into categories, each containing related articles. Articles can be tagged for cross-referencing. Teams can browse by category, filter by tag, or search for specific topics.

Guide categories

Categories include product areas (apparel, bags, drinkware, tech accessories, etc.) and operational topics (warehousing, shipping, decoration, program management). Each category contains multiple articles with practical guidance.

Use cases

  • Learning about different decoration methods and their trade-offs
  • Understanding product material options for specific use cases
  • Getting practical advice on shipping logistics across EU countries
  • Building internal knowledge about merch program best practices
