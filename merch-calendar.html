Merch Calendar

The Merch Calendar is a seasonal content module that helps teams plan corporate merch around key dates, events, and campaigns throughout the year.

What is the Merch Calendar?

The Merch Calendar provides timed, event-based content to help teams decide what merch to order and when. Instead of scrambling at the last minute, teams can browse seasonal recommendations, plan ahead, and align their merch orders with company events, holidays, and campaign cycles.

How it works

The Merch Calendar is organized around the calendar year. Each entry covers a specific season, event, or campaign theme with:

  • Product recommendations suited to the occasion
  • Timing guidance for ordering and production lead times
  • Decoration and branding suggestions
  • Use case examples from real company scenarios

Available languages

The Merch Calendar is available in English, German, and French.

Use cases

  • Planning Christmas and holiday employee gifts
  • Preparing summer conference and event kits
  • Scheduling onboarding pack waves for new hires
  • Coordinating seasonal office restocks
  • Campaign-specific merch for product launches or brand events
