Merch Atlas

The Merch Atlas is a curated knowledge hub that helps teams discover corporate merch products, understand categories, and find the right items for their use cases.

What is the Merch Atlas?

The Merch Atlas is a structured content library within the SoMerch platform. It organizes products, categories, and merch concepts into a browsable, searchable atlas - making it easier for teams to explore options without starting from scratch.

Each article in the Merch Atlas covers a specific topic, product category, or use case, with practical details, decoration options, and application notes.

How it works

The Merch Atlas is organized by tags and categories. Teams can browse the hub, filter by topic, or search for specific product types. Each article includes:

  • Product recommendations and category overviews
  • Decoration method guidance (screen print, embroidery, engraving, etc.)
  • Use case suggestions for different team sizes and event types
  • Links to related products and catalog items

Available languages

The Merch Atlas is available in English, German, and French - with articles localized for each market.

Use cases

  • Exploring product categories for a new merch program
  • Understanding decoration options before requesting a quote
  • Researching suitable products for a specific event or campaign
  • Sharing product knowledge across internal teams
