SoMerch FAQ

Common questions about SoMerch, managed merch operations, warehousing, fulfillment, and platform workflows.

What is SoMerch?

SoMerch is a software-powered corporate merch platform and service partner for distributed companies across Europe.

It helps teams plan, produce, store, and ship branded merch from one place.

Is SoMerch only a merch supplier?

No. SoMerch combines corporate merch services with software workflows for catalog browsing, quote requests, order tracking, inventory visibility, approvals, and fulfillment.

Who is SoMerch for?

SoMerch is designed for distributed companies, remote-first teams, companies with multiple offices, HR, operations, marketing, procurement, agencies, and studios.

Does SoMerch support multi-address shipping?

Yes. SoMerch is designed to support shipments to employees, offices, events, and partners across multiple addresses and countries.

Does SoMerch provide warehousing?

Yes. SoMerch can store and manage inventory for customers that need ongoing fulfillment, repeat orders, or distributed shipping.

Does SoMerch offer apps?

Yes. SoMerch is available on web, iOS, Android, and Windows.

Can I use SoMerch with ChatGPT?

Yes. SoMerch provides a ChatGPT plugin that connects through the public MCP server. In ChatGPT, add SoMerch as a connector with https://somerch.co/mcp and you can search products, plan merch projects, and request quotes in chat. See the ChatGPT integration guide →

Does SoMerch have a Zapier app?

Yes. SoMerch offers an official Zapier app, approved and listed on the Zapier Marketplace, with triggers for new orders, order status changes, shipments, and quote requests, plus searches and actions for products, orders, plans, and quotes. See the Zapier integration guide →

What other integrations does SoMerch offer?

SoMerch is building an integration ecosystem around the tools teams already use. Live today: the ChatGPT connector, the Zapier app, the public MCP server, and the public REST API. In review: the ChatGPT plugin directory listing, Microsoft Copilot, a Canva app, a monday.com app, and a Google Sheets app for the Google Workspace Marketplace. In development: Salesforce. See the integrations overview →

Is there developer documentation for the SoMerch API?

Yes. SoMerch publishes a complete interactive API reference and a machine-readable OpenAPI 3.1 specification covering every endpoint (OAuth, triggers, actions, webhooks, and resources), plus developer guides in the api-docs repository.

How do I contact SoMerch?

You can contact the SoMerch team through somerch.co or support@somerch.co.
